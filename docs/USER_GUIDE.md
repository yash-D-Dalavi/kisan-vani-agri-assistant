# Farmer and admin quick guide

**Farmer (web or WhatsApp):** send a voice note or text in Hindi or Marathi, for example "माझ्या कापसाची पाने पिवळी पडत आहेत". You get a spoken and written answer with the source. Prices and weather show their date. If the assistant is unsure it will ask you to repeat, ask which crop, or give the Kisan Call Centre number 1800-180-1551.
WhatsApp commands: "भाषा मराठी" / "भाषा हिंदी" to change language, "डेटा हटाओ" to delete your data.

**Admin:** open the *Documents* tab, enter your token if required, add a document (it stays unapproved), review it, then tick *Approved*. Use *Retrieval debugger* to see which chunks match a query. Use *Review queue* to turn refused conversations into test cases.

**Never use the assistant for:** pesticide mixes or doses it refuses to give, price predictions, or medical/legal advice. Always read the product label and confirm with your KVK.
